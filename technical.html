Technical Work

Engineering delivery grounded in analysis, manufacturability, and clarity.

This page summarizes the types of technical work I contribute to and the tools I use to turn ideas into reliable physical systems.

Design Engineering

Development of mechanical assemblies from concept sketches to production-ready models, emphasizing robust interfaces, serviceability, and controlled tolerance stacks.

Simulation & Validation

Structural and thermal analysis to verify assumptions early, reduce iteration cycles, and de-risk final design decisions before fabrication.

Prototype to Production

Rapid prototyping workflows paired with manufacturing feedback loops, ensuring designs hold up in machining, assembly, and maintenance.

Load Analysis

Systematic load-path tracing and margin-of-safety reviews to confirm that every structural joint satisfies service and ultimate load requirements.

Technical Documentation

Precise engineering drawings, specification packages, and review-ready reports that keep the entire team aligned from concept to sign-off.

Tooling

  • CAD: parametric part and assembly modeling
  • Analysis: static stress and fatigue assessments
  • Documentation: specifications, drawings, and review packs

Working Style

I prefer transparent engineering decisions and concise technical communication. Every choice is tied to a requirement: performance, reliability, manufacturability, or lifecycle cost.
